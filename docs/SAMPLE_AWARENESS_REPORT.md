## Interpretation

The seeded exercise illustrates how interaction rates can identify areas for practice. Clicks indicate engagement with a simulated lure; reports indicate a protective response. A person may report before or after clicking, so these are not mutually exclusive outcomes.

No learning-improvement percentage is claimed because the fresh fixture has no scored assessments. After a participant completes a baseline and follow-up, the learning dashboard reports the observed change in percentage points. The first baseline and latest follow-up are paired per participant and module.

## Privacy and controls

This report contains no named participant outcomes or email addresses. The application suppresses department-level results with fewer than three active participants. Administrative exports use keyed pseudonyms and require an administrator session; they are not anonymous research datasets.

The default retention window is 90 days, shortened by a campaign's configured policy. Expired enrollments and events are deleted together. The background sweep runs only while the service is running. Active identities and program configuration are retained separately as documented in README.

Simulation inputs remain local to the browser and are cleared on submit or exit and no outbound email transport. All data shown above is fixture data. A live campaign's printable report should be regenerated after the demonstration to reflect its actual interactions.
